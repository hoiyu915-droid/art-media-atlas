# Roadmap

Art Media Atlas is maintained incrementally. Priorities are ordered by validation and rights clarity rather than fixed dates.

## Near term

- Add machine-readable provenance and rights status for each distributable asset.
- Expand reviewed panel crops beyond the current representative set.
- Strengthen manifest, schema, link, checksum, and gallery validation.
- Publish reproducible setup instructions and tagged releases.

## Later

- Add community-submitted media comparisons with consistent test-board controls.
- Record public integrations and downstream uses without inventing adoption metrics.
- Stabilize resolver interfaces and deprecation rules for a 1.0 release.

Roadmap items are proposals, not promises. Material changes should begin with an issue and follow `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
